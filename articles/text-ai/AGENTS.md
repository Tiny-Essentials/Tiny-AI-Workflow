# text-ai/

Explainers about how text LLMs work, written for humans.

- `Basics-(pt-br).md` — entry point, Portuguese.
- `test-docs/` — drafts and model-comparison notes. Unpolished by design.

## Rules

- Audience: someone technical who has never trained a model.
- Explain the *why* before the *how*. No benchmark tables without context.
- Portuguese files must carry the `(pt-br)` suffix.
- Do not turn a draft into an article by deleting the caveats — move it and
  rewrite it.
