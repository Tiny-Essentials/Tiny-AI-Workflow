# AI User Archetypes

## A Field Guide to How People Engage with Generative AI

---

## 1. Hybrid Artist

This is the artist who is still making human art, but who takes the steps they struggle with most and uses AI to improve their own drawing — or any similar kind of project. This usually applies to lineart, parts of a video, music, and so on. It also covers generating assets to be placed separately into the project, either by using the raw AI file or a version redrawn by the artist, through the process of redoing everything their own way.

---

## 2. AI Art Artisan

This is the 100% human artist who is responsible for drawing (or doing any other art-related work for) AI model datasets. They sometimes work on creating large models, or they take commissions to help people create LoRA models. These LoRAs are usually dedicated to original characters (OCs) or to expanding the pose interpretation of existing models.

---

## 3. Semi-Artist

This is the user who knows very well how to manipulate AI to generate original content. Instead of relying on text prompts alone, they drive the generation through a manual, iterative loop: they paint, mask, correct, and repaint, evaluating each partial result before asking the model for the next step. This is usually done in software such as InvokeAI or similar tools with selection and painting features, which drastically reduces their dependence on prompts and gives them direct control over the final content.

The hardware does not define them. A Semi-Artist can do this work on a drawing tablet with full pressure sensitivity, or with nothing but a mouse and a lot of patience — the tool changes the comfort, not the method. What matters is that a human hand stays in the loop, making the decisions the model cannot make on its own.

These users are usually genuinely very creative, and they can accomplish many things that a Viber Art is normally unable to replicate. What will always separate them from the Viber Art is that they will always need to manually manipulate the AI to perform a specific task until they reach the final result of the project.

---

## 4. Viber Coding

This is the user who writes software by describing what they want to an AI and accepting the result without the ability to review, debug, or maintain it. They do not know the programming language, the framework, or the underlying concepts, and they do not treat the output as something that needs to be understood — only delivered. The name comes from the idea of programming by "vibe": the code either works or it does not, and the reason why is never part of the process.

The term was popularized in 2025 and, in its original sense, describes a legitimate and often productive workflow. What this entry describes is the extreme version: the person who ships code they cannot read, cannot secure, and cannot repair once the AI stops producing a working answer.

---

## 5. Viber Art

This has the same meaning as *vibe coding* (viber code). This is the prompter who does not know how to work in the artistic field they are acting in, and who also does not know how to use AI professionally to combine artistic skills with AI. They limit themselves specifically to typing prompts in the hope of seeing a good result.

---

## 6. Viber Slop

This is the person who uses AI exclusively to make AI slop on the internet, flooding the internet with useless content that brings no cultural value. It is the purest form of the person who only knows how to shitpost using AI.

---

## 7. The-AIR — The AI Revolutionary

This is the extremist user who, unlike a regular AI user, blindly believes that AI will replace all people, and who desires AI supremacy. This user sometimes does not even know how AI actually works, sometimes has never used a local AI, and — most notably — builds their experience only on big tech AIs.

---

## 8. Anti-AI

This user is **not** an AI critic, and you can feel that energy in the way they treat AI and the people around them who use AI. This is the inverse extremist of the The-AIR. They operate in the same way as the The-AIR, but in reverse: they often have never used an AI, they know AI only through big tech technologies, and so on. Their main difference is that they are the opposite, wishing for a supremacy that destroys AIs and exterminates them completely from all social and cultural means.

WARNING: Be very careful when labeling a person with this category, so that you do not end up fitting into the The-AIR category yourself.

---

## Quick Reference

| Archetype | Core Trait | Relationship with AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hybrid Artist** | Keeps human art, delegates weak steps | Tool for specific steps |
| **AI Art Artisan** | Builds datasets and LoRAs | Technical craft |
| **Semi-Artist** | Controls output with precision tools | Skilled operator |
| **Viber Coding** | Ships code they cannot read or fix | Hopeful input |
| **Viber Art** | Prompts only, no artistic control | Hopeful input |
| **Viber Slop** | Floods the internet with low-value content | Volume over value |
| **The-AIR** | Believes in AI supremacy | Ideological extreme |
| **Anti-AI** | Believes in AI extinction | Ideological extreme (inverse) |
